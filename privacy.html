Meggy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Meggy runs on your own devices: an iPhone app and a "brain" program on your own Mac. This policy explains what Meggy reads, where it goes, and how to remove it. Questions: wearemeggy@gmail.com.

What Meggy reads

  • Gmail (read only). For unread messages in your primary inbox from the last two days: the sender's name and the subject line. Meggy does not read message bodies or attachments, and cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Google Calendar (read only). Today's events: title, time and location.
  • Spotify. Your recently played tracks and top artists, and playback controls when you ask her to play something.
  • On your iPhone, only if you allow it: your calendar, Apple Reminders, location, photos you pick, the microphone while you hold Talk, and Apple Health. Health data never leaves the phone.
  • What you tell her. Conversations, reminders, ideas and places you save.

Where it goes

  • Account sign-ins happen on Google's and Spotify's own pages. Meggy never sees your password.
  • The long-lived sign-in key is stored in your Mac's Keychain, and only reaches the Mac over an encrypted connection. Everything Meggy remembers is stored on your Mac.
  • To write her replies, Meggy sends the conversation and a few lines of context (for example, "3 unread, the newest from a sender with this subject") to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service, under Anthropic's terms. Her voice is made by ElevenLabs from the text of her replies.
  • Meggy does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone else.
  • Data from Google and Spotify is never used to train AI models.

Google user data

Meggy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show you your mail and day in Meggy and to let her know about your day when you talk to her.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect any account in Meggy (Connections, Disconnect). Access is revoked with the provider and the key is deleted from your phone and Mac.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in Spotify's account settings.
  • Ask Meggy to forget anything she remembers, or delete her memory on your Mac.

Children

Meggy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
